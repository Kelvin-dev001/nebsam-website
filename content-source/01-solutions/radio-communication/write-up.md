# LONG-RANGE PoC RADIOS

## COMMUNICATE ACROSS CITIES, REGIONS & COUNTRIES

Long-Range Push-to-Talk over Cellular (PoC) radios use **4G/Wi-Fi networks** to enable voice communication over virtually any area covered by the underlying network.

Unlike conventional radio systems that are limited by their radio range, PoC communication allows teams operating in different cities, regions, or countries to communicate through the network.

### YOUR TEAM CAN BE FAR APART. YOUR COMMUNICATION DOESN'T HAVE TO BE.

For businesses operating across Kenya, East Africa, or international routes, PoC radios provide a powerful way to maintain centralized communication between teams in different locations.

# HOW LONG-RANGE PoC RADIO WORKS

## COMMUNICATION THROUGH 4G/Wi-Fi

### STEP 01 — CONNECT

The PoC radio connects to an available 4G/Wi-Fi network.

### STEP 02 — PUSH

The user presses the Push-to-Talk button.

### STEP 03 — TRANSMIT

The voice communication is transmitted through the network.

### STEP 04 — RECEIVE

The intended user or group receives the communication wherever they are connected to the supported network.

### RESULT

**Wide-area communication without conventional radio-distance limitations.**
