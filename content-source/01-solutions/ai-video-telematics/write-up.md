## THE HARDWARE

The system is a three-camera unit. **All three cameras record simultaneously** — the interior, front and rear views are captured at the same time rather than switched between.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Cameras | 3, recording simultaneously — interior, front and rear |
| Lens resolution | 720p per lens |
| Viewing angle — front | 110° |
| Viewing angle — interior | 130° |
| Viewing angle — rear | Not supplied — `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: rear lens viewing angle]]` |
| Operating system | Android 8.1 |
| Memory | 2GB RAM + 32GB |
| Storage expansion | microSD up to 256GB |
| Cellular | 4G |
| Wireless | WiFi |
| Audio | Two-way talk · voice recorder |

Two-way talk means a manager can speak to the driver through the unit, and the driver can reply. The voice recorder captures audio alongside video.

---

## ADAS — ADVANCED DRIVER ASSISTANCE SYSTEM

Road-facing detection. The system analyses the view ahead and alerts on:

* Forward collision
* Lane departure
* Headway monitoring
* Pedestrian detection
* Stop sign detection
* Speed limit plate detection

---

## DMS — DRIVER MONITORING SYSTEM

Cabin-facing detection. The system analyses the driver and alerts on:

* Phone call
* Seat belt
* Fatigue
* Smoking
* Distraction
* Yawning
* No driver
* Lens covered

**Lens covered** is the tamper detection: it reports when the cabin camera has been obstructed, which is the most common way a driver defeats a monitoring system.

---

## DBA — DRIVING BEHAVIOUR ANALYSIS

Motion-based detection, derived from the vehicle's movement rather than from the cameras:

* Harsh acceleration
* Hard braking
* Collision
* Sharp cornering
