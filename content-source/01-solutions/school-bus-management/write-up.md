Most of these questions are currently answered by a phone call to a driver.

# HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

## FROM THE SCHOOL GATE TO THE FRONT DOOR

---

## STEP 01 — DRIVER VERIFICATION & ALCOHOL SCREENING

### THE BUS DOES NOT MOVE UNTIL THE DRIVER IS CLEARED

Before the bus is allowed to leave the school compound, the assigned driver completes a breath test using the onboard alcohol detection sensor.

The system analyses the driver's breath to determine whether they are fit to operate the vehicle. Where intoxication is detected, the engine immobilization function prevents the bus from starting, according to the system's safety configuration.

To prevent an unauthorized person from taking the wheel after the test, the integrated vehicle video telematics camera performs a facial verification check immediately afterwards, confirming that the person who completed the screening is the driver assigned to that vehicle.

Only once the driver has been verified and cleared is the bus authorized to depart.

### SOBRIETY CHECK → IDENTITY CHECK → AUTHORIZED DEPARTURE

---

## STEP 02 — AUTOMATIC TRIP ACTIVATION

When the bus leaves the school compound, trip monitoring activates automatically.

Parents immediately receive a notification confirming that the bus has departed and that live tracking is now available in the mobile application.

**No one has to remember to start the trip. The journey starts itself.**

---

## STEP 03 — LIVE SCHOOL BUS TRACKING

Parents follow the bus's real-time location, movement and estimated arrival time from the mobile app.

Tracking sessions activate only during active pickup and drop-off operations — visibility when it matters, privacy when it doesn't.

---

## STEP 04 — PROXIMITY NOTIFICATIONS

As the bus approaches a student's pickup or drop-off point, the system automatically sends a proximity alert to the parent or guardian — for example, approximately five minutes before arrival, depending on configuration.

### NO MORE WAITING AT THE GATE. NO MORE MISSED PICKUPS.

---

## STEP 05 — AUTOMATED ATTENDANCE & BOARDING CONFIRMATION

When a student boards or exits the bus, attendance is recorded automatically through the onboard attendance management system.

The system can be configured to support:

* RFID cards
* Facial recognition
* Fingerprint recognition
* Iris recognition

Immediately after a successful boarding or alighting event, the parent receives an automated notification confirming the student's status, location and timestamp.

For younger children who cannot reliably operate an RFID system, the driver or bus attendant can capture attendance manually, marking each student as **picked up**, **dropped off** or **absent**.

### THE REGISTER UPDATES ITSELF, AND THE PARENT KNOWS WITHIN SECONDS.

---

## STEP 06 — END-OF-TRIP MONITORING

Once students have been safely dropped off and the journey is complete, the tracking cycle for that trip concludes automatically until the next scheduled route begins.

The same cycle repeats for the afternoon drop-off journey.

Authorized administrators can access the onboard vehicle video telematics system to monitor activity inside and around the bus in real time.

Alcohol breath sensors at the driver's cabin allow administrators to monitor driver alcohol status, supporting the school's policy that no driver operates a bus under the influence.

Administrators can:

* Create pickup and drop-off schedules

* Modify routes temporarily or permanently

## 08. EMERGENCY PANIC ALERT SYSTEM

Each bus is fitted with **two** emergency panic buttons:

* Driver cabin panic button
* Student compartment panic button

Either the driver or the students can raise an alert. The alert transmits immediately to the administration portal, enabling rapid response by school management.

### THE CHILDREN AT THE BACK OF THE BUS CAN RAISE THE ALARM TOO.

---

## 09. REPORTS & ANALYTICS

Access reports covering:

* Student attendance
* Bus utilization
* Route performance
* Driver activities
* Trip history
* Vehicle movement
* Safety incidents

Use the data to review service quality and improve transport operations term on term.

---

## 10. TRANSPORT FEE MANAGEMENT

### COLLECT TRANSPORT FEES WITHOUT CHASING PARENTS

The Transport Fee Management module lets schools manage, collect and track transport fees from one digital platform.

Administrators configure the fee structure from the admin portal according to the school's own policy, creating billing plans on **monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual** schedules and assigning the applicable fees to individual students or routes.

Once the tariff structure is configured, the school integrates its preferred payment gateway, allowing parents to pay securely through the Parent app.

Using the **Pay Now** feature, parents can:

* View outstanding balances
* Review payment history
* Pay transport fees from anywhere, at any time

The system also supports automated billing reminders and notifications for upcoming due dates, overdue payments, successful transactions and other fee-related updates.

### FEWER FOLLOW-UP CALLS. FASTER COLLECTION. CLEARER RECORDS.

In both cases:

* Attendance is recorded automatically
* Boarding and alighting times are captured

## Compliance notes for whoever builds the public page

**This page needs more legal care than any other page on the site.** The solution processes children's personal data, children's biometric data, and driver biometric and health-adjacent data (alcohol readings). Under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, biometric data is sensitive personal data, and Nebsam is a registered data controller and processor.

The public page must therefore:

- describe data handling plainly — what is collected, who can see it, how long it is kept
- state that biometric attendance methods are optional and that RFID or manual attendance can be used instead
- avoid implying that video from inside a school bus can be viewed by anyone other than authorized school administrators
- avoid any wording that promises absolute safety; the system supports safety, it does not guarantee it
- avoid presenting driver alcohol screening as a legal or evidential test
- link to the privacy policy, and the privacy policy must specifically address children's data and biometric data for this product
