# BAOFENG BF-888s SHORT-RANGE RADIO

## DIRECT RADIO-TO-RADIO COMMUNICATION FOR A SINGLE SITE

**A 16-channel UHF handheld operating on 420-450MHz with a 1500mAh lithium-ion battery, VOX hands-free operation, emergency alarm and PC programming.**

The Baofeng BF-888s is a conventional short-range two-way radio. It transmits directly to other radios on the same frequency, with no network, SIM card or data connection involved. That makes it the simplest and most self-contained option in the Nebsam radio range.

# HOW SHORT-RANGE RADIO DIFFERS FROM PoC

A short-range radio communicates directly with other radios within its operating range. It does not depend on cellular coverage, which is its advantage: it keeps working where there is no mobile signal at all.

The trade-off is distance. Operating range is limited by transmit power, terrain, obstructions and antenna, and the supplied document does not state a range figure for this model — `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Baofeng BF-888s operating range, open ground and obstructed]]`
