# INRICO T-290 PoC RADIO

## LONG STANDBY AND A LARGE SPEAKER, BUILT FOR NOISY ENVIRONMENTS

**A Push-to-Talk over Cellular handset with a 5200mAh battery supporting 30 hours of standby, a Ø36mm speaker for loud environments, an anti-interference microphone, front and rear cameras and an IP54 rating.**

The Inrico T-290 is a Push-to-Talk over Cellular (PoC) radio. It carries voice over a mobile data or Wi-Fi network rather than a conventional radio frequency. Its distinguishing characteristics in the Nebsam range are battery endurance and audio hardware built for noisy working environments.

### THE LONGEST STANDBY AND THE LARGEST SPEAKER IN THE RANGE.

---

# PoC OPERATION REQUIRES NETWORK COVERAGE

The T-290 communicates through an LTE, WCDMA, GSM or Wi-Fi connection. Where there is no network service, the handset cannot transmit or receive. On a PoC radio the limiting factor is network coverage, not radio range.

Communication across cities, regions and countries is possible subject to network coverage and service availability in each area of operation.
