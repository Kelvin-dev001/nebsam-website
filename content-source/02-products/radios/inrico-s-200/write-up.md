# INRICO S-200 PoC RADIO

## THE MOST FULLY SPECIFIED PoC HANDSET IN THE NEBSAM RANGE

**An Android 7.0 Push-to-Talk over Cellular handset with a 3.1-inch touch screen, 13.0MP rear camera, NFC, a 4000mAh battery rated at 40 hours standby and 12 hours talk time, and an IP54 rating.**

The Inrico S-200 is a Push-to-Talk over Cellular (PoC) radio built on Android. It carries voice over a mobile data or Wi-Fi network rather than a conventional radio frequency, and is the most completely documented handset in the Nebsam radio range.

### A RADIO, A CAMERA AND A CONNECTED TERMINAL IN ONE DEVICE.

---

# PoC OPERATION REQUIRES NETWORK COVERAGE

The S-200 communicates through an LTE, WCDMA, GSM or Wi-Fi connection. Where there is no network service, the handset cannot transmit or receive. On a PoC radio the limiting factor is network coverage, not radio range.

Communication across cities, regions and countries is possible subject to network coverage and service availability in each area of operation.

## Claims that must not be carried over

**"Unlimited distances without interference."** See the register in brief section 4.2. Range on a PoC device is bounded by cellular network coverage.

**"Waterproof: IP54."** IP54 denotes limited dust ingress protection and protection against water splashing from any direction. Publishing it as "waterproof" would overstate the rating and is the kind of specification error a technical buyer will catch immediately.
