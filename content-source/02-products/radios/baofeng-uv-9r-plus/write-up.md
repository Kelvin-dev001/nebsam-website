# BAOFENG UV-9R PLUS SHORT-RANGE RADIO

## IP67-RATED, DUAL-BAND, BUILT FOR WET AND DUSTY WORK

**A rugged dual-band handheld covering 136.00-174.00MHz and 400.00-520.00MHz, rated IP67 for dust and water ingress, with VOX hands-free operation and an emergency alert button.**

The Baofeng UV-9R Plus is the ruggedised model in the short-range range. It transmits directly to other radios with no network, SIM card or data connection involved, and its IP67 rating makes it the appropriate choice where equipment gets wet, dropped or covered in dust.

# INGRESS PROTECTION

The source document states the rating and what it means in practice:

> IP67 Dust & Waterproof or water resistant. It can work well under one-meter water for 30 minutes.

IP67 denotes complete protection against dust ingress and protection against temporary immersion. This is the only model in the Nebsam short-range range with a stated ingress rating.

## Items requiring verification before publication

1. `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Baofeng UV-9R Plus emergency alert behaviour]]` — **raise this one with the client explicitly.** The source describes the emergency button as transmitting "an alert siren followed by transmission of spoken or incidental sounds". In plain terms that means the radio may open its microphone and broadcast whatever it picks up, including conversation the user has not chosen to transmit. Whether that is configurable, how long it transmits for, and who receives it are questions a security or logistics buyer will ask, and there is a workplace-privacy dimension to describing it on a public page. Describe the behaviour accurately or not at all.
2. `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: CAK licensing for short-range frequency radios]]` — applies to all five short-range models. See the BF-888s notes.
3. `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Baofeng UV-9R Plus price]]` — no price in any supplied document.
4. `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Baofeng UV-9R Plus channels, battery capacity, transmit power, standby and talk time, speaker, display]]` — undocumented. Channel count is the notable gap: the BF-888s (16) and UV-5R (128) both state theirs, so a comparison table cannot be completed without it.

## Claims requiring care

**"IP67 Dust & Waterproof or water resistant."** The source's own wording is internally uncertain — "waterproof **or** water resistant". Use the rating, not the adjective: IP67 means dust-tight and protected against temporary immersion to one metre for thirty minutes. Do not publish "waterproof" unqualified.

**"Long battery life"** is a comparative with no baseline. Replace with the measured capacity once obtained.
