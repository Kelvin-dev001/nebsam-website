**A thin, lightweight 16-channel handheld covering 440-480MHz, with priority scan, selectable wide and narrow channel bandwidth, busy channel lockout and Windows programming and tuning.**

The Kenwood TK-3000 is a conventional short-range two-way radio. It transmits directly to other radios with no network, SIM card or data connection involved, and sits in the Nebsam range as the established-brand alternative to the Baofeng handhelds.

# HOW SHORT-RANGE RADIO DIFFERS FROM PoC

A short-range radio communicates directly with other radios within its operating range. It does not depend on cellular coverage, which is its advantage: it keeps working where there is no mobile signal at all.

The trade-off is distance. Operating range is limited by transmit power, terrain, obstructions and antenna, and the supplied document does not state a range figure for this model — `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Kenwood TK-3000 operating range, open ground and obstructed]]`
