# BAOFENG UV-82 DUAL BAND SHORT-RANGE RADIO

## DUAL PUSH-TO-TALK, TWO FREQUENCIES FROM ONE HANDSET

**A dual-band handheld with 128 programmable memory channels and a rocker PTT switch that lets the user transmit on either of two frequencies without changing channel, plus semi-duplex dual watch and a louder speaker.**

The Baofeng UV-82 is a conventional short-range two-way radio. Its distinguishing feature is the dual push-to-talk rocker: a supervisor can hold one frequency for their own team and another for site control, and choose which to transmit on with their thumb.

### TWO TEAMS, TWO FREQUENCIES, ONE THUMB.

---

# DUAL PUSH-TO-TALK AND DUAL WATCH

The dual push-to-talk switch allows the user to transmit on two frequencies using the rocker PTT.

The radio has one built-in receiver but can "watch" two channels in semi-duplex operation. It monitors two different frequencies — even on different bands, VHF and UHF — and gives priority to the first station to receive an incoming call.

The user can also listen to FM broadcast radio while still monitoring their radio frequencies in the background.
