# INRICO S-100 PoC RADIO

## GLOBAL-RANGE PUSH-TO-TALK WITH CAMERAS AND IP68 PROTECTION

**A network-connected Push-to-Talk handset with front and rear auto-focus cameras, accurate GPS, a dedicated SOS emergency alarm button, a 4000mAh battery and an IP68 protective design.**

The Inrico S-100 is a Push-to-Talk over Cellular (PoC) radio. It carries voice over a mobile data network rather than a conventional radio frequency, and adds camera, mapping and emergency-alert capability to standard Push-to-Talk operation.

### TALK, LOCATE AND RAISE THE ALARM FROM ONE HANDSET.

---

# PoC OPERATION REQUIRES NETWORK COVERAGE

The S-100 communicates through a mobile data or Wi-Fi network. Where there is no network service, the handset cannot transmit or receive. On a PoC radio the limiting factor is network coverage, not radio range.

Communication across cities, regions and countries is possible subject to network coverage and service availability in each area of operation.
