# 5 ADVANCED SECURITY LAYERS

## INTELLIGENT PROTECTION BEYOND ORDINARY TRACKING

The Hybrid Pro Tracker adds five advanced security mechanisms designed to detect unauthorized interference and help prevent vehicle theft.

---

# 01. ANTI-NETWORK JAMMING

### PROTECTION AGAINST GSM/GPS SIGNAL INTERFERENCE

Signal jammers can be used by thieves to interfere with vehicle communication and tracking systems.

The Hybrid Pro Tracker is designed to **detect GSM/GPS jamming attempts** and automatically activate vehicle immobilization according to its configured security logic.

**Detect the attack. Respond to the threat. Protect the vehicle.**

---

# 02. ANTI-TAMPER ALERT

### KNOW WHEN SOMEONE INTERFERES WITH YOUR TRACKER

If an unauthorized person attempts to open or tamper with the tracker, the system sends an **instant SMS notification to the owner**.

This allows you to know when someone is attempting to interfere with your vehicle security system.

**Unauthorized interference should never go unnoticed.**

---

# 03. ANTI-REMOVAL PROTECTION

### REMOVING THE TRACKER DOESN'T DEFEAT THE SYSTEM

The Hybrid Pro Tracker is designed to detect unauthorized tracker removal.

When illegal removal is detected:

* The vehicle can be automatically immobilized
* The engine remains disabled even after rewiring attempts
* Restoration requires a **unique recovery device**

This creates an additional barrier against thieves who attempt to locate and physically remove the tracker.

**Remove the tracker. The vehicle remains protected.**

---

# 04. ILLEGAL DOOR OPENING CALL ALERT

### KNOW THE MOMENT YOUR VEHICLE IS ACCESSED

When the vehicle is armed, the system detects unauthorized door opening and **automatically calls the owner**.

Instead of relying solely on a conventional alarm sound, you receive direct notification through your phone.

Whether you are at home, in the office, or away from the vehicle, you can be alerted to a possible break-in attempt.

**Your vehicle calls you when something isn't right.**

---

# 05. SMART BLUETOOTH IGNITION

### YOUR SMARTPHONE BECOMES PART OF YOUR VEHICLE'S SECURITY

The Smart Bluetooth Ignition system adds an additional authorization layer to engine starting.

The vehicle will not start until the owner's smartphone establishes an authorized Bluetooth connection using a **secure 4-digit PIN**.

The owner can:

* Enable or disable the feature
* Change the secret 4-digit PIN
* Authorize engine starting through the smartphone
* Maintain an additional security layer beyond the conventional vehicle key

### NO AUTHORIZED BLUETOOTH CONNECTION. NO AUTHORIZED ENGINE START.

This provides an additional layer of protection against unauthorized use of the vehicle, including situations where someone has access to the physical vehicle key.
