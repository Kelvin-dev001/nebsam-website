# 4 ADVANCED ANTI-THEFT SECURITY LAYERS

## PROTECTION THAT GOES BEYOND TRACKING

The Hybrid Tracker is designed to respond to several common vehicle theft and security threats.

---

# 01. ANTI-NETWORK JAMMING

### DETECT SIGNAL INTERFERENCE. RESPOND TO THE THREAT.

Thieves may attempt to use GSM/GPS jammers to interfere with vehicle communication and tracking.

The Hybrid Tracker is designed to **detect GSM/GPS jamming attempts and automatically activate vehicle immobilization according to its configured security logic.**

**Designed to keep your vehicle protected when someone attempts to interfere with its communication.**

---

# 02. ANTI-TAMPER ALERT

### KNOW WHEN SOMEONE INTERFERES WITH YOUR TRACKER.

If someone attempts to open or tamper with the tracker, the system immediately sends an **SMS notification to the owner.**

This provides an early warning that someone may be attempting to compromise your vehicle's security system.

**Suspicious interference shouldn't go unnoticed.**

---

# 03. ANTI-REMOVAL PROTECTION

### REMOVING THE TRACKER DOESN'T MEAN DEFEATING THE SYSTEM.

The Hybrid Tracker is designed to detect unauthorized removal.

When the tracker is illegally removed:

* The vehicle can be automatically immobilized
* The engine remains disabled even after rewiring attempts
* Restoration requires a **unique recovery device**

This creates an additional security barrier against thieves who attempt to physically locate and remove the tracking device.

**The tracker can be removed. The security lock remains.**

---

# 04. SMART BLUETOOTH IGNITION

## YOUR PHONE BECOMES PART OF YOUR VEHICLE'S SECURITY.

The Smart Bluetooth Ignition system introduces an additional authorization layer before the vehicle can start.

The engine will not start until the owner's smartphone establishes an authorized Bluetooth connection using a **secure 4-digit PIN.**

The owner can:

* Enable or disable the feature
* Change the 4-digit security PIN
* Authorize engine starting through the smartphone
* Add an additional layer of protection beyond the conventional vehicle key

### NO AUTHORIZED BLUETOOTH CONNECTION. NO AUTHORIZED ENGINE START.

This provides an additional safeguard against unauthorized use of the vehicle, even when someone has access to the physical vehicle key.
