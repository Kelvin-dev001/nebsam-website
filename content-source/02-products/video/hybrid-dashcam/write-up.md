# NEBSAM HYBRID DASHCAM

## DUAL-LENS RECORDING, LIVE TRACKING AND REMOTE VIDEO FROM ONE UNIT

**A dual-lens vehicle camera with live GPS tracking, cloud storage, remote video access from a phone app, vibration detection and a parking monitoring mode that keeps watching after the engine is off.**

The Nebsam Hybrid Dashcam is a self-contained vehicle camera. It records the road and the cabin through two lenses, stores footage in the cloud, reports its position live, and can be viewed remotely from a phone. It is aimed at owners and small fleets who want video evidence and vehicle visibility in a single unit.

# HYBRID DASHCAM OR AI VEHICLE VIDEO TELEMATICS?

**These are two separate products.** They are frequently confused, they are not interchangeable, and choosing the wrong one is an expensive mistake. This section exists to answer the question directly.

| | Hybrid Dashcam | AI Vehicle Video Telematics |
|---|---|---|
| Cameras | Dual lens | Three cameras recording simultaneously — interior, front and rear |
| What triggers a recording | Vibration, radar, touch and motion detection | AI analysis of the road, the driver and the vehicle's motion |
| Driver monitoring | Not documented | Driver Monitoring System — fatigue, distraction, phone use, smoking, seat belt, yawning, no driver, covered lens |
| Road hazard detection | Not documented | Advanced Driver Assistance System — forward collision, lane departure, headway, pedestrian, stop sign, speed limit plate |
| Driving behaviour analysis | Driving record | Harsh acceleration, hard braking, collision, sharp cornering |
| Parking monitoring | Yes | Not documented |
| Two-way talk | Not documented | Yes |
| KEBS permit | **Not covered** | Covered — Permit to Use the Standardization Mark SM#84618, STREAMAX brand |

**Choose the Hybrid Dashcam** when the requirement is video evidence, live location and remote viewing for a vehicle or a small fleet, including protection while parked.

**Choose AI Vehicle Video Telematics** when the requirement is to manage driver behaviour across a fleet, with automated in-cab alerts and the third-party laboratory evidence that comes with the KEBS permit.

They can be described together, but they must never be presented as the same product or as tiers of one product.

# WHAT EACH FEATURE MEANS IN PRACTICE

## LIVE GPS TRACKING

The unit reports its position, so the camera and the tracker are the same device rather than two installations.

## DUAL LENS CAMERAS

Two lenses record at once. The brochure does not state which directions they face, their resolution, or their field of view — `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Hybrid Dashcam lens orientation, resolution and viewing angles]]`

## PARKING MONITORING MODE

The unit continues to monitor the vehicle when it is parked. Together with radar, touch and motion detection, this is what distinguishes it from a camera that only records while driving.

## RADAR / TOUCH / MOTION DETECTION

Three separate detection methods trigger the unit. The brochure's own line is *"Get instant alerts when someone touches your vehicle"*.

## VIBRATION DETECTION

Impact and disturbance while parked or driving generate an event.

## REMOTE VIDEO AND CLOUD STORAGE

Footage is retained in the cloud and can be retrieved remotely rather than by removing a memory card. Retention period is not stated — `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Hybrid Dashcam cloud storage retention period and whether it is subscription-based]]`

## LOW POWER CONSUMPTION

Relevant to parking mode, where the unit draws from the vehicle battery with the engine off. No figures are given — `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Hybrid Dashcam power draw, and battery protection cut-off in parking mode]]`

A dashcam product page without a resolution figure will not rank and cannot be bought from with confidence.

## Items requiring verification before publication

1. `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Hybrid Dashcam full specification]]` — resolution, viewing angles, lens orientation, storage, retention, power, voltage, IP rating, night recording, dimensions. All eleven fields are open.
2. **4G — provenance resolved.** An earlier revision of this file recorded that "4G" appeared nowhere in the brochure. That was true of the *extracted text* but wrong about the document: the brochure's product photograph shows **"4G Dual Lens Car Camera"** and **"Built-in Radar"** printed on the device casing. The brochure therefore does evidence 4G, and independently corroborates the "Radar/touch/motion detection" feature bullet. 3. `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Hybrid Dashcam price and recurring cost]]` — no price in any supplied document, and cloud storage plus live tracking imply an ongoing cost that must be disclosed on the page, not at checkout (brief PART 10.2).
4. `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: Hybrid Dashcam cabin recording and privacy]]` — if either lens faces the cabin, the product records occupants. Under the Data Protection Act 2019 that has consequences for commercial and PSV use, and the page should say plainly what is recorded and who can view it.
5. `[[NEEDS_VERIFICATION: parking mode battery protection]]` — a unit drawing from the vehicle battery with the engine off risks a flat battery. Whether there is a low-voltage cut-off is a real buying question.
